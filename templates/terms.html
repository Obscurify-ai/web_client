1. Service Description

Obscurify.ai provides privacy-focused access to AI language models. We act as an intermediary between you and various AI providers, allowing you to use these services without directly exposing your identity to them.

2. Account Registration

You can chat as a guest without an account. Paid models and the API require an account, which is identified only by a random 16-digit account number. We store only a scrambled copy of the number and cannot recover it, so a lost number means a lost account and balance. Accounts created with a username and password must switch to their account number; password sign-in is being retired. You are responsible for:

  • Keeping your account number and API key safe
  • All activity that occurs under your account

3. Deposits and Account Balance

Our service operates on a prepaid credit system. You add funds by buying vouchers and redeeming them to your account balance, which then pays for AI model usage. Vouchers are blind-signed, so we cannot link a voucher to the payment that bought it.

An unredeemed voucher works like cash: anyone with its code can redeem it once, and we cannot replace a lost or stolen voucher. A redeemed voucher counts as a deposit from the time it is redeemed.

Deposit Expiration: All deposits expire five (5) years from the date of deposit. After this period, any unused balance from that deposit is forfeited and becomes the property of Obscurify. This policy helps us maintain operational sustainability while providing a generous timeframe for you to use your credits.

  • Deposits are non-refundable except where required by law
  • Account balances cannot be transferred to other users
  • Starter credit given to new accounts is promotional: it is not a deposit, has no cash value, and cannot be refunded or withdrawn. We may change or end it at any time, and give at most one per person
  • We reserve the right to suspend accounts with suspicious activity

4. Plans

We offer prepaid monthly plans with different features and pricing. A plan's fee is charged from your account balance when you choose it and again each month. If your balance can't cover the next month, your account moves to Pay As You Go. There are no card subscriptions.
