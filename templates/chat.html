Is there an AI I can use anonymously?

Yes. On Obscurify.ai you can open the page and chat with Llama 3.3 70B as a guest, with no sign-up, no email and no cookies. For the other 200+ models you need an account, which is a random 16-digit number instead of an email and password. Guests get 30 messages an hour, so we hold your IP in memory for up to an hour to count them.

Which AI is 100% private?

None in the cloud, and be wary of any service that says otherwise. The only way to keep a prompt entirely to yourself is to run the model on your own device. Obscurify.ai's Local Mode does that with small open models in your browser. In the cloud chat we scramble your identity before the request reaches the model provider and don't save your prompts or replies, but the provider still reads the prompt.

Is anonymous AI chat really anonymous?

Partly. Obscurify.ai scrambles your identity first, so the model provider can't tell who sent a prompt, but it does read the prompt itself. Leave out your name and anything else that points to you. Card payments are still visible to Stripe, and we keep IP addresses for up to 30 days to stop abuse.
