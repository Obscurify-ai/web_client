Anonymous Cloud AI

Obscurify.ai is an anonymous AI provider: your VPN for AI. Chat with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 300+ other models, uncensored ones included. Guests chat free with DeepSeek V4 Flash with no account, email or cookies, and an account is just a random code. Providers never see who you are, and conversations are held in memory for up to 1 hour for performance reasons, then permanently deleted. Pay with Monero, Bitcoin or card, or use the whole site over Tor.

Is there an AI I can use anonymously?

Yes. On Obscurify.ai you can open the page and chat with DeepSeek V4 Flash as a guest, with no sign-up, no email and no cookies. For the other 300+ models you need an account, which is a random 20-character code instead of an email and password. Guests get 30 messages an hour, so we hold your IP in memory for up to an hour to count them. Like every visit, it is also in our server logs, which are deleted after 30 days.

Which AI is 100% private?

None in the cloud, and be wary of any service that says otherwise. The only way to keep a prompt entirely to yourself is to run the model on your own device. Obscurify.ai's Local Mode does that with small open models in your browser. In the cloud chat we scramble your identity before the request reaches the model provider and don't keep your prompts or replies in any form we can read, but the provider still reads the prompt.

Is anonymous AI chat really anonymous?

Partly. Obscurify.ai scrambles your identity first, so the model provider can't tell who sent a prompt, but it does read the prompt itself. Leave out your name and anything else that points to you. Card payments are still visible to Stripe, and we keep IP addresses for up to 30 days to stop abuse.
