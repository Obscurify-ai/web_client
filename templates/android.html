Obscurify for Android

A small, fast app for your Obscurify account. Sign in with your account number, chat with hundreds of models, and keep your chats only on your phone.

Download the APK

Android 7 or newer. About 5 MB, downloaded from GitHub.

Install

  1. Open this page on your phone and tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks you to allow your browser to install apps; allow it.
  3. Tap Install, then sign in with your account number or create a new one.

Updates

The app doesn't update itself. To get new versions automatically, install Obtainium and add https://github.com/Obscurify-ai/obscurify-android. Or come back here and install the new APK over the old one; your chats stay.

An F-Droid listing is on the way.

What's in It

  • Account number sign-in, no email or password. The app keeps only the session, never the number.
  • Streaming answers, web search, image attachments and collapsible reasoning.
  • Search models by category: Free, Cheap, Reasoning, Uncensored, Roleplay and Frontier.
  • Add funds with blind vouchers by card, Bitcoin, Lightning or Monero.
  • Remote images in answers become links, so they never load by themselves and reveal your IP.

Check the Signature

Every release is signed with the same key. The SHA-256 of its certificate is:

7C:A4:01:A8:FE:D2:19:C1:7F:19:7A:F6:B0:7C:10:D0:63:09:7F:B6:39:98:90:B5:8B:58:D5:8C:D8:72:A7:1C

Compare it with AppVerifier on the phone, or run apksigner verify --print-certs obscurify.apk. Android also refuses an update signed with any other key. The source and every release are on GitHub.
