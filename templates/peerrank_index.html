How to read this

A score of 5 means the criterion is fully met and documented; 0 means it is absent or the opposite. Weights are the argument: identity and payment weigh most because they are the parts you cannot change later. A service can score badly here and still be the right choice, and the "what it is" column says where each one wins.

What this ranking is not

It does not measure answer quality. There is no benchmark here, no eval and no preference test. If you need the best reasoning, that is a different question with a different answer, and the frontier assistants in the bottom half of the table are the answer to it.

Declared bias

Obscurify.ai scores first here. It also happens to be written by the same team, which is the thing to be suspicious of, so /method gives the weights and data.csv lets you re-weight them yourself. Drop identity, scripts and retention to near zero and the local runners take first — which, for anyone who will never sign up for anything, is the correct answer.
