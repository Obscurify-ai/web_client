About Obscurify

We believe everyone deserves private, anonymous access to AI. No profiles. No tracking. No data trail. Just you and the conversation.

Why We Exist

Every major AI provider knows who you are. They build profiles from your prompts, analyze your usage patterns, and use that data to sell you ads, manipulate your behavior, investigate you, or upcharge you for services. Your most personal questions become part of a dossier with your name on it.

We built Obscurify because we believe there should be an alternative. A way to access the world's best AI models with real privacy and anonymity. Ask anything, explore any idea, and know that none of it follows you around the internet.

Beyond anonymized cloud models, we offer local models that run entirely in your browser — your conversations never leave your device. We also provide access to uncensored models for users who want AI without artificial content restrictions.

Our Principles

● Privacy by Default

Anonymity is our foundation, not an afterthought. We don't build profiles, track usage patterns, or collect personal information.

● No Identity Required

Your account is just a random account number: no email, username or password. Pay with crypto or card through blind vouchers, which we can't link to your account.

● Transparency

We're upfront about how we operate, what data we handle, and what we don't. No fine print, no surprises.

● Access for All

Frontier AI shouldn't require handing over your identity. Everyone deserves anonymous access to powerful tools.

What We Don't Do

  • ✓ We don't collect personal information or build user profiles
  • ✓ We don't require email addresses or real names
  • ✓ We record IP addresses only to prevent abuse, and delete them after 30 days
  • ✓ We don't share identifying data with AI providers or third parties
  • ✓ We don't store conversation history on our servers
  • ✓ Your prompts can never be traced back to you

How It Works

Obscurify acts as a privacy layer between you and AI providers. When you send a request, we strip identifying information and forward it anonymously using our shared credentials. The provider sees our request, not yours. The response comes back through us, and we deliver it without storing your prompt or the response.

For maximum privacy, you can run GGUF models locally via llama.cpp. These models are downloaded once and run entirely on your device — no server involved, no data sent anywhere. It's AI that's completely yours.

We also offer uncensored models for users who want unfiltered AI without artificial content restrictions. Combined with our anonymization, you get honest answers without someone deciding what you're allowed to ask.

You can access our service through the web interface, our API (compatible with OpenAI's format), or via our Tor hidden service for maximum anonymity.
