Method

Nine criteria, fixed weights, 0-5 per criterion, 100 points total. The weights are the argument and they are published so you can disagree with them numerically.

Last revised {{ as_of }}. Previous versions in corrections.

Why weight at all

An unweighted list of features is worse than a weighted one, because it pretends every criterion matters equally. They don't. Whether a service knows who you are cannot be changed after you sign up; whether it has an onion address can be worked around. So identity, payment and retention carry the most points, and price carries few: price is the easiest thing to change.

Rules

  • One source per score, named. Each service's row cites the page the score came from and the date it was checked. If nothing could be checked, the score is 0 and the source says so.
  • No vendor briefings. Nobody has been interviewed, so no service has had the chance to explain a score away.
  • Only public pages count. A policy that exists but is undocumented scores as absent.
  • "Not found" is not "no". Where an onion address was looked for and not found, the row says not found, and the score reflects the absence from the reader's side.
  • Rechecked on a schedule. Every row on a 90-day clock. A change to a policy or price is a correction, not a new edition.

What is deliberately excluded

Answer quality. There is no benchmark, no eval set and no preference test behind this table, which is the biggest single omission and the honest reason to read a frontier-assistant list alongside it. Also excluded: uptime, because it changes weekly and would date every row.

Known weaknesses

  • The author is scored. Obscurify.ai is written by the same team. It ranks first. /data.csv exists so you can re-weight and check whether that survives.
  • Local runners are awkward. Jan and Ollama have no server, so identity, scripts and retention are trivially satisfied. Their payment, Monero and Tor columns are marked down to reflect that you are not buying anything from them and the onion question is moot. They are included because leaving them out would be the bigger distortion.
  • Thirteen services is not a census. It is the set that came up repeatedly, plus two local runners.

Disagreements

A score that is wrong with a source attached gets changed, and the change is recorded in corrections with the reason. A weight you would set differently is not a correction; re-weight the data instead.
