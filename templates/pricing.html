How billing works

Casual and Pro include a dollar amount of usage each month, spent at the same per-token prices everyone pays, on any model you choose. Once it's used, and on Pay As You Go, usage is charged from your balance. See prices for every model.

Top up your balance with blind vouchers, bought with Monero, Bitcoin or Lightning through our own BTCPay Server, or by card. We can check a voucher is genuine but can't tell which payment it came from (how blind payments work). Plans are prepaid: they renew monthly from your balance while it covers them.

Deposits are non-refundable and expire five years after they're made. See the Terms of Service.
